03 · How it works

From request to conversation.

Four steps. Nothing is scheduled or charged until our team has confirmed the details with you.

Choose an officer

Name the one you'd prefer, or let us suggest based on your questions.

Send your questions

A short form — your situation and what you want to discuss.

Confirm a time

Our team contacts you about availability and the session terms.

Meet on Zoom

A private conversation on a link we set up for you.

Sending a request is not a confirmed booking. Our team contacts you about the officer's availability and the session terms first. Payment is arranged after that — the form itself never charges you.

After the session, if learning support comes up and you agree, our team can point you to relevant SAT or AP tutoring. It never happens automatically.

04 · Before you request

Questions people ask first.

The short version of the terms. Anything else, ask in the form — we answer before scheduling.

01Is this a separate service from tutoring?+
Yes. An admissions conversation is its own paid service. You don't need tutoring to request one, and a session never commits you to anything else.
02Who will I actually talk to?+
The officer you name in your request, or one we suggest from your questions. We confirm who it will be before a time is set.
03What happens after I send a request?+
Our team contacts you about the officer's availability and the session terms. Once you're happy, we set a time and send the Zoom link. Until then, nothing is booked and nothing is charged.
04Can I ask for a specific officer?+
Yes — the form has a "preferred expert" field. Availability is confirmed afterwards; if your first choice can't take a session soon, we'll say so and suggest an alternative.
05Is the conversation recorded?+
Nothing is recorded or transcribed automatically. If any recording were ever proposed, it would be with your explicit consent, agreed separately in advance.
06What if I also need help with a subject?+
Tutoring is a separate request. If a session shows that subject support would help and you'd like it, our team can point you to relevant SAT or AP tutoring — only when you ask.
