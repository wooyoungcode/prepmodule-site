Current grade

  1. Grade 9Grade 9Now

    Explore and build habits

    • Take the most challenging courses you can do well in
    • Try activities, and notice what you keep coming back to
    • Start a simple record of activities and awards

  2. Grade 10Grade 10Now

    Go deeper

    • Narrow to one or two interests and take on responsibility
    • Plan junior-year courses — rigor matters most next year
    • Try practice tests to choose between the SAT and ACT

  3. Before grade 11Before grade 11Now

    A summer with substance

    • A program, research project, job or community work
    • Something you can explain in one sentence
    • Light, steady test prep

  4. Grade 11Grade 11Now

    The year that counts most

    • The strongest grades of high school
    • Leadership in the activities you chose
    • Take the PSAT/NMSQT in October
    PSAT · Oct

  5. Grade 11Grade 11Now

    Testing and a first list

    • SAT or ACT, leaving time for a retake
    • AP exams in May
    • A first school list; ask two teachers for recommendations
    AP · May

  6. Before grade 12Before grade 12Now

    Essays and the application

    • Draft the personal statement
    • Finalize the list: reach, target and likely schools
    • The Common App opens on August 1
    Common App · Aug 1

  7. Grade 12Grade 12Now

    Applications go out

    • Early Action and Early Decision — often November 1 or 15
    • Regular Decision — usually January 1–15
    • Keep grades strong: colleges see senior-year grades

Before you request

Questions people ask first.

The short version of the terms. Anything else, ask in the form — we answer before scheduling.

01Is this a separate service from tutoring?+
Yes. An admissions conversation is its own paid service. You don't need tutoring to request one, and a session never commits you to anything else.
02Who will I actually talk to?+
The officer you name in your request, or one we suggest from your questions. We confirm who it will be before a time is set.
03What happens after I send a request?+
Our team contacts you about the officer's availability and the session terms. Once you're happy, we set a time and send the Zoom link. Until then, nothing is booked and nothing is charged.
04Can I ask for a specific officer?+
Yes — the form has a "preferred expert" field. Availability is confirmed afterwards; if your first choice can't take a session soon, we'll say so and suggest an alternative.
05What if I also need help with a subject?+
Tutoring is a separate request. If a session shows that subject support would help and you’d like it, our team can point you to the right tutoring — only when you ask.
