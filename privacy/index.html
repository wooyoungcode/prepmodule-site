Privacy Policy

How we handle information for enquiries and educational services, and your privacy rights.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Review draft · confirm privacy contact, retention and overseas processing before publication

Privacy contact: info@prepmodule.com. Website requests are forwarded to our team through FormSubmit. You may also use email or the linked WhatsApp channel.

PrepModule is operated by Ayajin (아야진), represented by Jaejun Park (박재준), business registration number 268-06-03283. This policy covers website visits, enquiries and the administration of lessons and consultations for students and parents.

Contact info@prepmodule.com for privacy enquiries or requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of consent. Our business address is 68-18 Bamgol-gil (Gyo-dong), Sokcho-si, Gangwon State, Republic of Korea.

3. Collection and current enquiry channels

We process information you send through the website form, email or messenger, information you provide during a service, and lesson and invoicing records entered by authorised staff. We rely on an applicable lawful basis, such as consent, steps needed to arrange or perform a contract, or a legal obligation.

When submitted, the form’s name, email, student grade, time zone, service choices, message and consent are forwarded through FormSubmit to info@prepmodule.com. Our team uses them to respond and discuss availability. FormSubmit’s official documentation states that it retains submissions for 30 days. KakaoTalk enquiries are coming soon; email and the linked WhatsApp channel are also available.

You may decline required information or consent, but then this form cannot be submitted or answered. Other enquiry channels still require the minimum information needed to reply. Optional information or marketing consent is not required to use the core service. Do not include national identification numbers, health information or other unnecessary sensitive details in an ordinary enquiry.

4. Retention

We retain information for the period needed to answer the enquiry or provide the service. When it is no longer needed, or following a valid deletion or withdrawal request, we check any legal retention obligation and delete it without undue delay.

Proposed operating rules for review: up to one year after an enquiry is resolved if it does not lead to a contract; and up to one year after a service ends for routine lesson, consultation and learning records. These periods require confirmation against the operator’s actual practices.

For transactions subject to Korea’s Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, contract and withdrawal records are kept for five years, payment and service-supply records for five years, consumer complaints and disputes for three years, and advertising records for six months. Tax or other statutory records are kept separately for the legally required period and not used for unrelated purposes.

Hosting and authentication providers retain their own security logs under their policies. Those periods are separate from the enquiry and service records managed by Ayajin.

5. Deletion

We identify records for deletion after checking the retention period, purpose and legal duties. Electronic files are deleted to prevent recovery where practicable; paper records are shredded or destroyed.

We check related copies in email, working documents, private administrator storage and downloaded files. Legally required records are separated with restricted access. Backups are not used for routine business and are deleted through their relevant retention cycle.

6. Sharing with experts and third parties

We do not sell information or provide it for unrelated third-party advertising. We limit information shared with an assigned tutor or admissions expert to what is needed to deliver the service.

Where separate consent is required for an independent third-party disclosure, we first explain the recipient, purpose, information, retention and effect of refusing. Sharing with an expert outside Korea is also subject to any required notice and consent. Valid legal requests are handled only within the scope permitted by law.

External font and image requests may reveal IP, browser and request information to the provider. If you choose to visit WhatsApp or another external service, its policy also applies. Merely visiting this website does not send your enquiry content to a messenger.

8. Processing outside Korea

Our global hosting, email and authentication providers may process information outside Korea. We verify an applicable legal basis and notice requirements, such as separate consent or processing and storage needed to arrange or perform a contract.

Contact info@prepmodule.com about overseas processing, withdrawal or restriction. You can use email instead of an external messenger. If a requested restriction prevents us from providing a service, we will explain the effect.

Pre-publication checks: the actual Supabase project storage country; processing locations, processor contact details and specific overseas retention periods for the Google business account and FormSubmit. These details have not been confirmed. This draft must not be treated as the final public policy until those checks are complete.

9. Browser storage and access information

The public website uses the localStorage value pm-lang to remember your language preference. You can clear it in your browser’s site-data controls; you may then need to choose a language again.

The administrator area keeps authentication information in tab sessionStorage and clears it on sign-out. Clearing browser data or closing the tab removes it according to browser behaviour, and you may need to sign in again.

The current public website code does not include Google Analytics or advertising tracking pixels. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. External resource providers process access information under their respective policies.

10. Your rights, representatives and children

You or an authorised representative may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of consent. Email info@prepmodule.com with your request and a way to reply. We perform proportionate identity checks and respond under applicable law.

If a legal retention duty prevents us from fully completing a request, we explain the reason and available steps. You may refuse processing, although refusing information needed for an enquiry or service can prevent us from responding or delivering it.

Where consent is the basis for processing information about a child under 14, we obtain and verify a parent’s or legal guardian’s consent. A parent or guardian should email us first. If necessary consent cannot be verified and no other lawful basis exists, we delete the child’s information.

11. Security

Only an approved Google account can access the administrator interface. The database and file store also check permissions. Lesson and invoicing records and PDFs are private and are not given public file URLs.

We use HTTPS for transmission and limit access to authorised operating staff. Operational records are separated from public website files. Sign-out clears the visible operational information and authentication for that session.

12. Privacy contact, remedies and updates

Proposed privacy contact for review: Jaejun Park, Ayajin’s operating representative. The designation requires confirmation. Contact info@prepmodule.com for privacy issues and access or deletion requests.

For external assistance in Korea, contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118 within Korea) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

We publish policy changes and their effective date on this page. If a change of purpose or other processing requires additional notice or consent, we complete that process first.
