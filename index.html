04 · The team

A team that listens before it recommends.

We run this service out of our work with students and families preparing for US universities. The people you'll meet — former admissions officers and subject tutors — are introduced with their real background, and nothing is assigned to you without a conversation first.

  • Every profile states the person's role and verified experience.
  • Tutors are suggested only after our team has heard your goals.
  • Your request is a conversation starter, not a booking or a charge.
01Can I buy just one service?+
Yes. Admissions conversations and tutoring are separate services with separate requests. You can use either one on its own.
02How are tutors recommended?+
After you send a request, our team talks with you about your subject, level, learning style, and schedule. We then suggest about three tutors and explain why each one fits. You choose.
03Is everything online?+
Yes. Admissions sessions happen on a Zoom link we prepare, and tutoring is held online. Time zones are confirmed with you before anything is scheduled.
04Do I need an admissions session before tutoring?+
No. You can request tutoring directly. If you do have an admissions session and learning support comes up, we'll point you to the relevant tutoring — but never automatically.
