04 · Before you request

Questions people ask first.

The short version of the terms. Anything else, ask in the form — we answer before scheduling.

01I need help with more than one AP subject.+
Choose one subject on the form to start and mention the others in the notes. Our team works out with you whether one tutor or two makes more sense. There's no multi-subject checkout here on purpose — it's a conversation.
02Is tutoring separate from the admissions sessions?+
Yes. Tutoring is its own service with its own request. You don't need an admissions session first, and having one never commits you to tutoring.
03Does the tutor follow my school's course or the exam?+
Both, and you set the balance. Most students start with school work and shift toward exam format in the spring. Tell us where your class is and when your exam is; the shortlist reflects that.
04Where do lessons happen, and what about time zones?+
Online, on a video platform confirmed with you. We shortlist only tutors whose hours overlap with yours.
05What happens after I choose a tutor?+
Our team confirms the schedule and the lesson terms with you — including how changes and cancellations work — before the first lesson. Nothing starts until you've agreed to those.
06Is there a free trial lesson?+
We don't promise anything here that hasn't been confirmed. If a trial or a particular pricing arrangement applies, our team tells you before you commit — not the website.
