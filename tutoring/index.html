How we work

Consult. Match.
Teach. Report.

Every student goes through the same four steps — so nothing is assigned without a conversation, and parents are never left guessing.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We start by listening: the student’s goals, current level, school and exam board, how they learn best, and what parents want to see.

  2. 02

    Matching

    We match the student with the tutors who fit best, with the reason for each. You choose.

  3. 03

    Personalized lessons

    Lessons follow a plan made for this student: school pace, exam dates, weak spots, and any project or mentoring goals.

  4. 04

    Tutoring reports

    Parents get regular reports: what was covered, how the student is doing, and what comes next.

Before you request

Questions people ask first.

The short version of the terms. Anything else, ask in the form — we answer before scheduling.

01Can I get help with more than one program or subject?+
Yes. Put the one that matters most on the form and mention the others in your message. Our team may suggest one tutor for both, or a different tutor for each.
02Will the tutor know my exam board or IB level?+
That's part of the match. Tell us HL or SL for IB, and the board — Cambridge, Edexcel, AQA, OCR — for A-Level and IGCSE. If you're not sure, the course name your school uses is enough.
03What’s in a tutoring report, and who gets it?+
What was covered, how the student did, what comes next, and a short note from the tutor. Reports go to parents and the student; how often they arrive is agreed when lessons are set up.
04Is tutoring separate from the admissions sessions?+
Yes. Tutoring is its own service with its own request. You don't need an admissions session first, and having one never commits you to tutoring.
05Where do lessons happen, and what about time zones?+
Lessons are online, on a link our team sets up. Your time zone goes on the request form, and the schedule is confirmed with you before the first lesson.
06What happens after I choose a tutor?+
Our team confirms the schedule and the lesson terms with you — including how changes and cancellations work — before the first lesson. Nothing starts until you've agreed to those.
