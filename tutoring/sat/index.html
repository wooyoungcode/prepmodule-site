02 · What we ask

Tell us where you need support.

Four things shape the shortlist. The form asks for them; nothing is scored or ranked automatically.

Area

Reading & Writing, Math, or both. If you're not sure which is costing more points, say so.

Current level

A recent score, a practice test, or "haven't taken one yet" — all fine.

Goal

A target score, a test date, or a deadline the score has to be ready for.

How you learn

Explanation style, pace, and the time zone and hours you can actually attend.

Any score or improvement figures on this site come from verified records only. We don't publish averages we can't stand behind.

04 · Before you request

Questions people ask first.

The short version of the terms. Anything else, ask in the form — we answer before scheduling.

01Is tutoring separate from the admissions sessions?+
Yes. Tutoring is its own service with its own request. You don't need an admissions session first, and having one never commits you to tutoring.
02How are the three tutors chosen?+
From your area, current level, goal, learning style, and available hours. Our team writes a reason next to each suggestion so you can compare on something concrete.
03Where do lessons happen, and what about time zones?+
Online, on a video platform confirmed with you. Tutors and students are in different time zones — we shortlist only tutors whose hours overlap with yours.
04What happens after I choose a tutor?+
Our team confirms the schedule and the lesson terms with you — including how changes and cancellations work — before the first lesson. Nothing starts until you've agreed to those.
05Can I ask for a specific tutor?+
Yes. Naming one on the form records your interest, and we'll include them if they're available and a fit. It isn't a guaranteed assignment.
06Is there a free trial lesson?+
We don't promise anything here that hasn't been confirmed. If a trial or a particular pricing arrangement applies, our team tells you before you commit — not the website.
